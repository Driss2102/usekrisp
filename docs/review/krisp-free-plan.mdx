# Krisp free plan

<DisclosureNotice />

**Krisp does not have a permanent free plan anymore.** The only no-cost way to use it today is a 7-day free trial that unlocks every premium feature, with no credit card. After the 7 days you pick a paid plan. If you remember Krisp having a "free forever" tier, that is gone.

## What changed

For years Krisp offered a genuinely useful free plan: a daily allowance of noise cancellation you could use indefinitely at no cost. Krisp has since moved to a trial-based model. The current pricing page lists a 7-day free trial and three paid plans, and Krisp's own FAQ confirms noise cancellation is bundled into every plan rather than sold as a standalone free tool.

So the honest state in 2026 is simple: there is a free trial, not a free plan.

## What the 7-day free trial includes

During the trial you get full access, not a stripped-down demo:

- **Unlimited noise cancellation** for the trial period, in both directions.
- **Unlimited transcription.**
- **Unlimited AI notes and action items.**
- **No credit card required** to start.

It resets nothing and hides nothing for those 7 days. It is the real product, so it is the best way to judge whether the noise cancellation is worth paying for.

## What happens after the trial

When the 7 days end you choose a paid plan. Krisp's plans are Core, Advanced, and Enterprise, and noise cancellation is included in all of them. Prices change and vary by region, so we do not quote a figure here. See the current numbers on our [pricing breakdown](/docs/review/krisp-pricing/) or [Krisp's pricing page](/docs/review/krisp-pricing/).

## What's actually free vs paid

| Option | Cost | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Built-in filters (Zoom, Teams, Discord) | Free forever | Basic, one-directional noise removal |
| **Krisp 7-day trial** | **Free, no card** | Full Krisp: two-way noise cancellation plus AI notes, for 7 days |
| Krisp paid (Core / Advanced / Enterprise) | Paid | Full Krisp, ongoing |

The only *permanently* free noise removal is the basic filter inside your calling app. Krisp itself is free only for the 7-day trial, then paid.

## If you specifically want something free forever

If a paid plan is not an option, there are genuinely free routes, but they are weaker than Krisp:

- **Built-in suppression in your calling app.** Discord, Zoom, and Google Meet each ship a basic noise filter. It is free and always on, but one-directional and lighter than Krisp. See [Discord: Krisp vs standard](/docs/compare/krisp-app-vs-discord/).
- **NVIDIA Broadcast**, free if you own an RTX GPU, for outgoing mic cleanup only. See [Krisp vs NVIDIA Broadcast](/docs/compare/krisp-vs-nvidia-broadcast/).

None of these match Krisp's two-way cleaning across every app, but they cost nothing.
