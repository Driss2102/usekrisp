# Is Krisp Pro worth it?

<DisclosureNotice />

**Krisp Pro is worth it if you hit the free plan's daily limit, and probably not if you do not.** The upgrade removes the caps and adds unlimited AI notes and recording, so the value depends entirely on how much you use it.

## What Krisp Pro unlocks over free

- **Unlimited noise cancellation**, instead of the free plan's 60 minutes per day.
- **Unlimited AI summaries and action items**, instead of a couple of notes per day.
- **Meeting history and recording.**
- **Compliance features** such as HIPAA, on the right plan.

The free plan already includes unlimited transcription, so Pro is mostly about removing the daily caps. See the full [free plan breakdown](/docs/review/krisp-free-plan).

## Who should upgrade

- You are on calls most of the day, so 60 minutes of noise cancellation is not enough.
- You rely on AI notes for more than a couple of meetings a day.
- You need recording, history, or compliance for work.

## Who should stay free

- You take a handful of calls a week.
- You mainly want the noise gone and do not lean on the notes.

Prices change and vary by region, so we do not quote a number. Check current plans on the [pricing page](/docs/review/krisp-pricing), and note that annual billing is cheaper per month.
