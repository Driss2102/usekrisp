<DisclosureNotice />

## Choose Krisp if…

Clean audio is the priority and you want no bot in the meeting. Krisp cleans both directions and captures bot-free. Its 17 transcription languages cover the common cases.

## Choose Notta if…

You transcribe across a wide range of languages and that breadth is the deciding factor. Notta supports far more transcription languages than Krisp.

## Or use both

Krisp for clean audio at the source, Notta where you need its wide-language transcription. They do not conflict.
