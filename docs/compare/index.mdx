# Best Krisp alternatives (2026)

<DisclosureNotice />

Krisp is our top pick for plug-and-play noise cancellation, but "alternatives" can mean two different things, so it helps to split them:

## If you want noise cancellation

- **NVIDIA Broadcast / RTX Voice.** Excellent noise removal, but it needs an NVIDIA RTX GPU and is not bidirectional. Great for streamers with the right hardware. See [Krisp vs NVIDIA Broadcast](/docs/compare/krisp-vs-nvidia-broadcast).
- **Built-in app suppression** (Zoom, Teams, Discord). Free and fine for light noise, weaker on heavy noise, and usually one-directional.

## If you want AI meeting notes

- **Otter, Fireflies, Fathom, tl;dv, Notta.** These go deeper on transcription, search, and integrations than Krisp's notes. If notes are your main job, they often win. See [Krisp vs Otter](/docs/compare/krisp-vs-otter), [Krisp vs Fireflies](/docs/compare/krisp-vs-fireflies), [Krisp vs Fathom](/docs/compare/krisp-vs-fathom), [Krisp vs tl;dv](/docs/compare/krisp-vs-tldv), [Krisp vs Notta](/docs/compare/krisp-vs-notta), [Krisp vs Jamie](/docs/compare/krisp-vs-jamie), and [Krisp vs Fellow](/docs/compare/krisp-vs-fellow). For a hardware recorder alternative, see [Krisp vs Plaud](/docs/compare/krisp-vs-plaud).

## Our take

If your core problem is noise on calls, Krisp is hard to beat and the 7-day free trial makes it risk-free to try. If your core problem is rich, searchable meeting notes, pair Krisp for audio with a dedicated note-taker, or pick one of the note-first tools above.
