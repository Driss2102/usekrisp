# Krisp for podcasters

<DisclosureNotice />

The hardest audio to fix in a podcast is a remote guest in a noisy room. Krisp helps by cleaning that noise in real time, at the source, before it ever reaches your recording. That means less to repair later and a guest who sounds present, not muffled by their surroundings.

## Where Krisp helps

- **Remote guest calls:** clean each side of the conversation live, so a noisy guest does not sink the episode.
- **Any recording setup:** Krisp sits at the system level, so its clean audio feeds whatever you record with.
- **Bidirectional:** it cleans both your voice and the incoming guest audio.

## Where a post-production tool fits better

Be honest with yourself about the job. If you already have a recorded file full of noise, a dedicated post-production tool built for cleaning existing audio will do more than a real-time tool can. Krisp's strength is preventing the noise during the recording, not restoring a finished file. Many podcasters use Krisp live, then a post tool only if something still needs work.
