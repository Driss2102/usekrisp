## Quick habits that help

- **Find a corner or a wall** to put some of the noise behind you.
- **Use a headset with a close mic** so it favours your voice.
- **Lower your voice** rather than raising it, and mute between turns.

These reduce the problem, but a coffee shop or an open-plan office will always leak chatter, music, and clatter into your mic.
