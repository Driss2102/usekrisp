## Choose Zoom's built-in if…

You live entirely in Zoom, the only problem is your own background noise, and you do not want to install anything. It is free, on by default, and handles light-to-moderate noise on your side.

## Choose Krisp if…

You take calls in more than one app, a noisy person on the other end keeps derailing calls, or you want AI notes and accent conversion that work the same everywhere. <AffiliateLink href={KRISP_AFFILIATE_URL} variant="inline" location="inline-intro">Krisp</AffiliateLink> cleans both directions and runs on top of any app, not just Zoom.

## Or use both

They do not conflict. Some people leave Zoom's suppression off and let Krisp handle audio across every app for one consistent setup, while others keep Zoom's on as a fallback.
