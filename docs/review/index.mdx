# Krisp Review (2026)

<DisclosureNotice />

Krisp is a voice AI app that sits between your microphone and any calling app, stripping out background noise, echo, and even the voices of other people in your room before they reach the call. Over the last few years it has grown from a pure noise-cancellation tool into a wider "voice assistant" that also transcribes meetings and writes AI notes.

This is an independent review. We are not Krisp, and we earn a commission if you start a plan through our links, at no extra cost to you. Here is where Krisp shines, where it falls short, and who should actually use it.

## The short version

Krisp does one thing better than almost anyone: **two-way noise cancellation** that works on top of Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Discord, and regular phone calls without you switching apps. The processing runs on your device, so audio does not leave your machine for cleanup. The meeting-notes side is convenient but not as deep as dedicated note-takers like Otter or Fireflies.

## Noise cancellation: the main event

This is the feature that made Krisp famous, and it is still the reason to use it. Two things set it apart:

- **Bidirectional cleaning.** It removes noise from *your* microphone and from *incoming* audio, so a noisy participant on the other end stops derailing the call.
- **App-agnostic.** Krisp registers a virtual microphone and speaker. You pick "Krisp Microphone" inside Zoom, Teams, Discord, or any app once, and it just works everywhere after that.

In practice it handles keyboard clatter, fans, traffic, cafe noise, and background chatter well. It is the closest thing to a "mute the world" button for calls.

## AI meeting notes and transcription

Krisp also records, transcribes (currently 17 languages), and generates AI summaries with action items. This is genuinely handy if you just want a quick recap attached to the tool you already run for noise cancellation.

That said, if AI notes are your main need, dedicated tools go deeper. Krisp's summaries are solid for a recap, but power users who live in their meeting notes may find them thin. We cover this in detail on our [Krisp alternatives](/docs/compare/) page.

## Free trial vs paid plans

Krisp no longer has a permanent free plan. What it has instead is a genuinely useful 7-day free trial, and that is the main reason it is easy to try:

- **7-day free trial:** full access to the premium features, including unlimited noise cancellation and AI notes, with no credit card.
- **Paid plans (Core, Advanced, Enterprise):** noise cancellation is in all of them; higher tiers add unlimited accent conversion, admin controls, and security and compliance features (HIPAA).

## Who Krisp is for

- Remote and hybrid workers on back-to-back video calls.
- Anyone in a noisy space: shared offices, cafes, homes with kids or pets.
- Streamers and Discord users who want clean audio without a hardware setup.
- Call centers and support teams (Krisp has a dedicated product for this).

## Who should look elsewhere

- People whose primary need is deep, searchable meeting notes with rich integrations. See our comparisons with Otter and Fireflies.
- Anyone who needs transcription in a less common language. Krisp supports 17 languages, so check that its current list covers yours before relying on it.
- Users who only ever use one app that already has strong built-in noise suppression.
