## Choose Krisp if…

Your problem is how you sound: background noise, a noisy room, or a noisy person on the other end. Granola does nothing about audio quality. Krisp cleans it in both directions, on any app, on Windows or Mac.

## Choose Granola if…

Your problem is note quality and you are on a Mac. Granola's you-type-it-enhances-it approach produces notes many people find sharper than a generic auto-summary, and it captures without a bot.

## Or use both

They do not conflict, and they pair well: Krisp for clean audio, Granola for the notes. Run Krisp as your mic and let Granola take the notes.
