# Best AI meeting assistant (2026)

<DisclosureNotice />

**There is no single best AI meeting assistant, because they are built for different jobs. If deep, searchable notes are the goal, Otter, Fireflies, and Fathom lead. If clean audio on the call matters as much as the notes, Krisp is the pick, since it is the only one here that removes background noise in both directions.** Here is the honest breakdown.

## What to look for

- **Notes depth:** how good the summary, action items, and search are.
- **Audio quality:** does it also make you sound clear, or only take notes?
- **Bot or bot-free:** does a visible bot join the call?
- **Where it works:** one platform, or any calling app.

## The picks, by job

- **Best for clean audio plus notes: Krisp.** The only tool here that cleans your microphone and the incoming audio in real time, on any app, and then takes AI notes. If noise is part of your problem, nothing else on this list solves it.
- **Best for deep, searchable notes: Otter.** Strong live transcription and searchable, structured notes. See [Krisp vs Otter](/docs/compare/krisp-vs-otter/).
- **Best free note-taker: Fathom.** A genuinely generous free tier for recordings and summaries, though it joins some calls as a bot. See [Krisp vs Fathom](/docs/compare/krisp-vs-fathom/).
- **Best for team-wide notes and CRM: Fireflies.** Deep search and integrations across a team. See [Krisp vs Fireflies](/docs/compare/krisp-vs-fireflies/).
- **Best for recorded, clippable meetings: tl;dv.** Built around shareable clips. See [Krisp vs tl;dv](/docs/compare/krisp-vs-tldv/).

## How to choose

- If your calls are noisy, start with **Krisp**, because clean audio is a problem the note-first tools cannot fix, and you can add a note-taker later.
- If your audio is already fine and you only need great notes, pick one of the note-first tools above by depth, budget, and whether you mind a bot.
- Many people run **Krisp for audio and a note-taker for the notes**, since they do not conflict.
