# Krisp for online teachers and tutors

<DisclosureNotice />

When you teach online, your voice is the lesson. Background noise, a noisy student, or a bad connection all get in the way of learning. Krisp keeps the audio clean in both directions and can turn each session into a transcript students can revisit.

## Why it fits teaching

- **Clear delivery:** remove household and street noise so students hear every word.
- **Cleaner student audio too:** Krisp cleans incoming audio, so a noisy student does not derail the class.
- **Automatic transcripts and summaries:** the AI Note Taker turns a lesson into a structured record students can study from, without a bot joining.
- **Works on your tools:** Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, and any other app you teach on.

## Set up before your next lesson

Install Krisp, enable noise cancellation, and pick Krisp as your microphone in your calling app. See our guides for [Zoom](/docs/use/zoom) and [Google Meet](/docs/use/google-meet).
