# Is Krisp Pro worth it?

<DisclosureNotice />

**A paid Krisp plan is worth it if you are on noisy calls often, and probably not if you only take the occasional quiet call.** "Krisp Pro" is the common shorthand for Krisp's paid plans, which today are called Core, Advanced, and Enterprise. There is no permanent free plan anymore, so the real question is whether to keep paying after the 7-day free trial.

## What the paid plans get you

Noise cancellation is included in every paid plan. Moving up the tiers mainly adds controls, integrations, and security:

- **Core**, for individuals and small teams: unlimited AI note-taker, unlimited noise cancellation, integrations, the mobile app, and multilingual transcripts.
- **Advanced**: unlimited accent conversion, advanced admin controls, more integrations, and extra storage.
- **Enterprise**: SSO and SCIM, advanced security and reporting, on-device private transcription, and HIPAA compliance.

## Who should pay

- You are on calls most of the day and clean audio matters on nearly every one.
- You rely on AI notes and recording for more than the odd meeting.
- You need admin controls, security, or compliance for work.

## Who can skip it

- You take a handful of quiet calls a week, so the free suppression built into your calling app is enough.
- You mainly want the noise gone and do not lean on the notes or integrations.

Prices change and vary by region, so we do not quote a number. Check current plans on the [pricing page](/docs/review/krisp-pricing/), and note that annual billing is cheaper per month.
