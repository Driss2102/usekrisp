# How to cancel a Krisp subscription

<DisclosureNotice />

**To cancel Krisp, open your account billing settings and cancel the plan there; if you subscribed through the App Store or Google Play, cancel it in that store instead.** Your paid features stay active until the end of the period you already paid for. Here is the full process, plus what happens to your notes.

## Cancel from your Krisp account

1. Sign in to your account at krisp.ai.
2. Open **Settings**, then the **Billing** or **Subscription** section.
3. Choose **Cancel subscription** (or downgrade), and confirm.
4. You keep the paid features until the current billing period ends, then the account reverts to the free trial's limits.

## If you subscribed on mobile

If you started Krisp through an app store, the billing is handled there, not in Krisp:

- **iPhone or iPad:** Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Krisp > Cancel.
- **Android:** Play Store > Profile > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions > Krisp > Cancel.

## Before you cancel

If the price is the issue rather than the product, two things are worth a look first:

- **Annual billing** is cheaper per month than monthly. See the [ways to save](/docs/review/krisp-discount/).
- If Krisp is not the right fit, our [alternatives shortlist](/docs/compare/) covers the closest tools by job, from noise cancellation to note-takers.

## What happens to your data

Cancelling stops future billing. It does not, by itself, delete your account or existing notes and transcripts. If you want your data removed, that is a separate account-deletion request in your settings. Check Krisp's current help centre for the exact data-retention terms.
