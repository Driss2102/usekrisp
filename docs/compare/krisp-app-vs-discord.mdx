# Discord: Krisp vs Standard (and do you need the app?)

<DisclosureNotice />

<CompareSelector current="discord" />

In Discord's voice settings, **pick Krisp over Standard**: it removes more background noise. But Discord's built-in Krisp only cleans your microphone, inside Discord. If you also take calls in other apps, want the noise gone from what you hear, or want meeting notes, the standalone Krisp app does more.

## Discord Krisp vs Standard: which should you pick?

Pick **Krisp**. Discord's Voice and Video settings give two noise-suppression options, Standard and Krisp. Krisp is the more advanced AI model, and it is the same technology Discord licenses: Krisp's Voice AI powers audio on more than 200 million devices and is used by companies including Discord (per Krisp's own product summary, 2026). It strips more keyboard, fan, and room noise than Standard.

When Standard makes sense: Krisp's AI uses more of your CPU. On an older or low-powered machine, or if your own voice starts sounding clipped, switch to Standard.

## What Discord's built-in Krisp does, and does not do

Discord's built-in Krisp runs on your **microphone, inside Discord**. It cleans the noise you send into a channel. It does not:

- work in other apps (Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, your browser, or games outside Discord)
- clean the audio you hear from other people
- record, transcribe, or take notes

## When the standalone Krisp app is worth it

The standalone Krisp app adds three things the built-in version cannot:

- It adds a virtual microphone and speaker, so the same noise removal works on top of Zoom, Teams, Meet, Webex, and any other calling app, not just Discord.
- It is **bidirectional**: it removes background noise from your microphone and from the other person's incoming audio.
- It includes an AI Note Taker that records without a bot joining, then generates transcripts, summaries, and action items.

## Who should use which

- **Only chat and game on Discord, and only need your own mic cleaned:** Discord's built-in Krisp is enough, and it is free. Set noise suppression to Krisp and move on.
- **Take calls in other apps too, want cleaner incoming audio, or want meeting notes:** the standalone Krisp app is the better fit. Its 7-day free trial gives you full access with no credit card, so you can test it before paying.
