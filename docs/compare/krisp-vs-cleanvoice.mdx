## Choose Krisp if…

You want to sound clean on live calls: Zoom, Teams, Meet, Discord, or the phone. Cleanvoice cannot help during a call. Krisp cleans your audio in real time, in both directions.

## Choose Cleanvoice if…

You already recorded something, such as a podcast or an interview, and you want to strip noise and cut the filler words, stutters, and dead air afterward. That editing is exactly what Cleanvoice is built for.

## Or use both

They fit different steps and pair well: <AffiliateLink href={KRISP_AFFILIATE_URL} variant="inline" location="inline-body">Krisp</AffiliateLink> for clean audio on the live call, Cleanvoice to tidy any recording you export afterward.
