## Choose Google Meet's built-in if…

You are already on a paid Google Workspace plan that includes noise cancellation, you only ever meet in Google Meet, and you just need your own mic cleaned. In that case it is already there at no extra cost.

## Choose Krisp if…

You are on the free Meet, you call in more than one app, incoming noise is the problem, or you want AI notes without a Workspace upgrade. Krisp works everywhere, on any plan, and cleans both directions.

## Or use both

They do not clash. If your Workspace plan includes it, you can keep Meet's cancellation for Meet and let Krisp cover every other app plus incoming audio.
