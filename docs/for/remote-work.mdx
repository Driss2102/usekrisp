# Krisp for remote and hybrid work

<DisclosureNotice />

Working from home means calls from a kitchen, a bedroom, or a cafe, with all the noise that comes with them. Krisp lets you sound like you are in a quiet office, on any app, and it can capture the meeting so you are not typing notes while trying to listen.

## Why it fits remote and hybrid teams

- **Sound professional from anywhere:** clean out kids, pets, traffic, and housemates in real time.
- **Works on the tools you already use:** Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Slack, and any other calling app.
- **Stay aligned across time zones:** the AI Note Taker records decisions and action items, so people who missed a call can catch up.
- **Hybrid friendly:** it can capture in-person meetings too, not just remote ones.

## Get set up in minutes

Install Krisp, enable noise cancellation, then pick Krisp as your microphone in each app. We have step-by-step guides for [Zoom](/docs/use/zoom), [Microsoft Teams](/docs/use/microsoft-teams), [Google Meet](/docs/use/google-meet), and [Slack](/docs/use/slack).
