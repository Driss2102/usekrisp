**Remote work did not reduce meetings, it multiplied them, and noise makes every one of them harder.** Here is a curated set of 2026 figures on how many meetings people sit through, how tired they are, how much time is wasted, and how much noise costs focus. Sources are listed at the end, and the noise figures name their source inline. Numbers are drawn from third-party reports and are best read as directional, not precise. Last updated: September 2026.

## Key statistics at a glance

- Remote workers attend about **25.6 meetings per week**, roughly **80% more** than fully in-office workers.
- **49%** of employees report virtual meeting fatigue.
- **47%** of knowledge workers say office noise stops them focusing (Jabra, 2024).
- **69%** of employees are dissatisfied with the noise at their workspace (Softdb, 2025).
- Wasted meeting time reached about **five hours per week** per worker.
- It takes about **23 minutes** to regain deep focus after an interruption (Gloria Mark, UC Irvine).

## How many meetings people attend

- Remote workers attend about **25.6 meetings per week**, compared to **14.2** for fully in-office workers, roughly **80% more**.
- Hybrid workers land in between, at around **20 meetings per week**.
- Remote workers average about **7.3 video calls per week**, versus **2.6** for in-office staff.

## Meeting fatigue is widespread

- **49%** of employees report experiencing virtual meeting fatigue.
- **61%** of remote workers say they feel mentally drained after consecutive video meetings.
- **76%** of workers agree they feel drained on days packed with meetings.
- Burnout is higher for remote workers (**42%**) than hybrid (**29%**) or in-office (**31%**).

## Noise is a top productivity drain

Meetings are only half the story. The noise around them quietly taxes focus, and the research is consistent.

- **47%** of knowledge workers say office noise prevents them from focusing, and **63%** struggle to concentrate because of it (Jabra, 2024).
- **70%** of employees report being regularly disturbed by conversations and ambient noise (Gensler Research Institute, 2024).
- **69%** of employees are dissatisfied with the noise at their primary workspace (Softdb, 2025).
- **37%** think noise-cancelling tools should be standard work equipment (Jabra, 2024).
- After a noise-driven interruption, it takes an average of about **23 minutes** to get back into deep focus (Gloria Mark, UC Irvine).

## Time is being wasted

- Wasted meeting time roughly **doubled between 2019 and 2024**, reaching about **five hours per week** per worker.
- Meetings miss their intended goal around **72%** of the time.

## What to take from this

The pattern is clear: more meetings, more fatigue, more noise, and a lot of wasted time. You cannot delete everyone's calendar, but you can make each meeting cost less. Two levers help most:

- **Fewer, tighter meetings**, with an agenda and a clear owner. See our guide on [running effective remote meetings](/blog/run-effective-remote-meetings).
