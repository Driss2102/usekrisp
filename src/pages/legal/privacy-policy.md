# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 August 2026._

Krisp Guide is a content website. We aim to collect as little data as possible.

## Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics (such as Google Analytics) to understand which pages are useful. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.

## Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to run the site and, where enabled, to measure anonymous traffic. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

## Affiliate links

When you click an affiliate link, the destination service (for example Krisp via Impact.com) may set its own cookies to attribute a referral. Those cookies are governed by that service's privacy policy, not ours.

## Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us via the [contact page](/contact).
