# Krisp on Microsoft Teams

<DisclosureNotice />

Teams has built-in noise suppression, but Krisp is stronger and works both ways. Setup takes a minute.

## Step by step

1. Install and open Krisp, set your real mic and speaker inside it, and turn noise cancellation on.
2. In Teams, open **Settings > Devices**.
3. Under **Microphone**, select **Krisp Microphone**. Under **Speaker**, select **Krisp Speaker**.
4. Start a call and confirm your audio is clean.
