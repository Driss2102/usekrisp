# Krisp discount and coupons

<DisclosureNotice />

**The most reliable way to pay less for Krisp is annual billing, and you can try everything first with the 7-day free trial.** Krisp also runs occasional seasonal promotions. We do not post a typeable coupon code, because the honest ways to save below are the ones that actually apply at checkout.

## 1. The free trial (try before you pay)

Before paying anything, run the 7-day free trial: full access to noise cancellation, transcription, and AI notes, with no credit card. It will not save you money long term, but it stops you paying for a tool that does not fit. See the full [free plan breakdown](/docs/review/krisp-free-plan).

## 2. Annual billing beats monthly

If you do upgrade to Pro, paying annually works out cheaper per month than paying monthly. This is the most reliable way to lower the price, and it is always available. We do not quote the exact figure here because prices drift and vary by region, so check the current numbers on [Krisp's pricing page](/docs/review/krisp-pricing).

## 3. Seasonal promotions

Like most software, Krisp tends to run promotions around events such as Black Friday and the new year. These come and go, so the move is to check the pricing page when you are ready to buy rather than wait indefinitely for a deal.

## How to make sure a discount applies

Start from our link and follow it to Krisp's site, then pick annual billing if you upgrade. Any active promotion is applied on Krisp's checkout, so you do not need a code from us.
