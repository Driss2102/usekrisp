# Krisp for call centers and BPOs

<DisclosureNotice />

Krisp sells a dedicated contact center product that is separate from the app individuals use for their own meetings. It targets the specific problems of high-volume phone support: noisy agent environments, hard-to-understand audio across accents, and the need for compliance and analytics at scale.

## What Krisp does for a contact center

- **Cleans agent audio both ways.** Bidirectional noise cancellation removes noise from the agent's side and the customer's side, which cuts repetition and misheard details.
- **Accent conversion.** Clarifies agent speech in real time while keeping the agent's natural voice, aimed at global support teams.
- **Voice translation.** Live translation for cross-language support (Krisp shipped a v3 of this in 2026).
- **Analytics and agent assist.** Speech analytics, agent coaching artifacts, and an enterprise analytics portal (rolled out in 2026). Feature availability depends on the deployment.
- **Compliance.** Supports SOC 2, PCI DSS, and HIPAA, depending on plan and configuration.

For the latest additions, see our [What's new in Krisp](/docs/changelog) page, where the recent release notes are mostly contact-center focused.

## Who it fits

Support and sales call centers, BPOs, and any team running high call volumes where audio clarity, accent handling, and compliance matter. This is an enterprise, deployment-dependent product, so pricing is handled through Krisp's sales team rather than the self-serve plan.
