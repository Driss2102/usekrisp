# Krisp for streamers and gamers

<DisclosureNotice />

Mechanical keyboards, fans, and a mic close to your face are a recipe for a noisy stream. Krisp strips that out in real time before it hits Discord, OBS, or your capture software, without a hardware chain or a treated room.

## Why streamers use Krisp

- **Removes keyboard clatter and fan noise** while you talk, so your voice stays front and center.
- **Works across your whole setup:** the same clean audio flows into Discord, OBS, and any recording tool, because Krisp sits at the system level.
- **No special hardware required.** It runs on a normal laptop or PC.

## Quick setup

1. Install and open Krisp, set your real mic and speaker, and enable noise cancellation.
2. In **Discord**, set your input device to **Krisp Microphone**.
3. In **OBS**, add an Audio Input Capture using **Krisp Microphone**.

## When NVIDIA Broadcast is the better pick

If you have an NVIDIA RTX graphics card and only need to clean your outgoing voice, NVIDIA Broadcast is a strong free option. Krisp wins when you are on a laptop, do not have an RTX card, or want the same clean audio on calls too. See our [Krisp vs NVIDIA Broadcast](/docs/compare/krisp-vs-nvidia-broadcast) comparison.
