## Free fixes to try first

- **Get the mic closer.** A headset mic near your mouth hears far less of the room than a laptop mic across the desk.
- **Pick a better spot.** A corner, soft furnishings, and a closed door absorb sound. Hard, empty rooms echo.
- **Turn on your app's built-in suppression.** Zoom, Teams, and Meet all have a basic noise filter in their audio settings. It helps with light noise.
- **Mute when you are not talking.** Obvious, but it stops the worst interruptions.

These get you part of the way. They do nothing, though, about noise that arrives while you are speaking, or the noise on the other person's end.
