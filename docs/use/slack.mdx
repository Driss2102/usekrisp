# Krisp on Slack

<DisclosureNotice />

Slack huddles are quick, which is exactly why nobody wants to fiddle with audio settings before one. Krisp fixes that once: it adds a virtual microphone that Slack uses, so every huddle is clean without extra steps.

## Step by step

1. Install and open the Krisp app, set your real microphone and speaker inside it, and turn noise cancellation on.
2. In Slack, open **Preferences**, then **Audio & video**.
3. Set your **Microphone** to **Krisp Microphone**.
4. Start a huddle. Your keyboard, fans, and room noise are now filtered out.

## Beyond huddles

Krisp also connects to Slack for its AI Note Taker, so meeting summaries and action items can be shared into a channel. That keeps the clean-audio tool and your team's workspace in one flow.
