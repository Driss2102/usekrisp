## Choose Krisp if…

Your problem is how you sound on calls. Read.ai does nothing about background noise. Krisp cleans it in both directions, on any app, and adds accent conversion.

## Choose Read.ai if…

You want meeting analytics: who spoke, engagement, sentiment, and a summary that ties into your other tools. That is Read.ai's specialty, not Krisp's.

## Or use both

They solve different problems and pair fine: Krisp for clean audio, Read.ai for the analytics and recap.
