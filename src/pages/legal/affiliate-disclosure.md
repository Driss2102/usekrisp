# Affiliate Disclosure

Krisp Guide is an independent website. We are not owned by, operated by, or officially affiliated with Krisp Technologies Inc.

## How it works

- We include referral links to products and services throughout our guides, tutorials, and comparisons.
- If you click a link and then sign up, subscribe, or make a purchase, we may earn a commission.
- The price you pay is the same whether you use our link or navigate directly.
- Attribution is tracked via cookies, with windows that vary by program (typically 30 to 90 days).
- Each affiliate program has its own terms, commission rates, and payout conditions. Consult the official terms of each program for specifics.

## Our editorial independence

Affiliate relationships do not determine what we write or how we rate tools. Our editorial process is:

- **Honest:** we describe limitations and failure cases, not just upsides.
- **Balanced:** when a competitor is better suited for a task, we say so, including when that means steering away from a higher-commission option.
- **Source-verified:** pricing and feature claims are checked against official documentation, not taken from marketing copy.

We earn our primary affiliate revenue from Krisp. This gives us a financial incentive to write thorough, accurate Krisp content, not to inflate its capabilities or suppress alternatives. Misleading guides drive away the readers this site depends on.

## What your support funds

Commission revenue from affiliate links goes toward:

- Hosting and infrastructure costs.
- Time spent researching, testing, and writing guides.
- Keeping all content free and publicly accessible.
- Creating new tutorials and updating existing ones as tools change.

## Sponsored and guest content

We currently have no sponsors. If an article is ever a sponsored placement or a guest post, it will be clearly labeled at the top of that article, and any current sponsors will be listed in this section. Sponsors do not control our editorial coverage of their products elsewhere on this site.
