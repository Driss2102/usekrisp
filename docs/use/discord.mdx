# Krisp on Discord

<DisclosureNotice />

Discord actually ships with a lightweight "Krisp" noise suppression built in, but the standalone Krisp app is more powerful and works across every app, not just Discord.

## Step by step

1. Install and open the Krisp app, set your real mic and speaker, and enable noise cancellation.
2. In Discord, go to **User Settings > Voice & Video**.
3. Set your **Input Device** to **Krisp Microphone**.
4. Tune **Input Sensitivity** so Discord picks up your voice but ignores your keyboard and room noise.
