# Krisp not working? Step-by-step fixes

<DisclosureNotice />

**Most Krisp problems come from three things: the wrong input device is selected, your CPU is overloaded, or an app does not have microphone permission. Work through the checklist below in order and it usually clears in a couple of minutes.**

## Quick checklist

1. Make sure Krisp is **running** and noise cancellation is toggled **on**.
2. In Krisp, confirm your **real microphone and speaker** are set as the devices Krisp listens to.
3. In your calling app, set the **input** to **Krisp Microphone** (and output to **Krisp Speaker**).
4. Close heavy apps if your **CPU** is maxed out.

## "Krisp Microphone" is missing or not detected

If your calling app does not show Krisp Microphone:

- Restart Krisp, then fully quit and reopen the calling app so it re-reads the device list.
- Reconnect the device: if you switched headsets or docked your laptop, reselect your real mic inside Krisp, then reselect Krisp Microphone in the app.
- On Windows, check **Settings > Privacy and security > Microphone** and make sure microphone access is on for your apps.

## High CPU (Krisp cuts out)

Krisp does its noise cleaning on your device, so under heavy load some apps will disable it to protect performance. If Krisp keeps dropping:

- Close CPU-heavy apps (games, video editors, lots of browser tabs) and test again.
- Lower other real-time effects running at the same time.
- On older machines, expect Krisp to need more headroom than a basic mic passthrough.

## Crackling, robotic, or distorted audio

- **Update your audio drivers.** Outdated or corrupt drivers are the most common cause of crackling.
- **Test wired instead of Bluetooth.** Bluetooth headsets can introduce artifacts; a wired connection tells you if Bluetooth is the culprit.
- **Turn off duplicate processing.** If your OS or calling app is also applying its own noise suppression, disable one of them so they do not fight.

## Still not working

- Sign out and back in, or reinstall Krisp to reset its virtual devices.
- Confirm your plan is active if a paid feature stopped, since the [free trial](/docs/review/krisp-free-plan/) reverts after 7 days.
- Check Krisp's status page and help centre for a current outage.
