## Choose Krisp if…

You want to sound clean on live calls: Zoom, Teams, Meet, Discord, or the phone. Adobe Podcast cannot help during a call. Krisp cleans your audio in real time, in both directions.

## Choose Adobe Podcast if…

You already have a recording, such as a podcast episode or a voice memo, and you want to strip its background noise and improve the voice after the fact. That is exactly what Enhance Speech is for, and it is free.

## Or use both

They fit different steps and pair perfectly: Krisp for clean live calls, Adobe Podcast to polish any recording you export afterward.
