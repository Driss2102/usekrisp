## Choose Krisp if…

You are on Windows, you switch devices, or you want more than noise removal: incoming audio cleaned too, AI notes, and accent conversion. Krisp covers all of that across platforms.

## Choose Utterly if…

You are on a Mac, you only need your own mic cleaned, and you want the cheapest way to do it. Utterly is focused and inexpensive, and the local processing keeps audio on your machine.

## Or use one, not both

Unlike the note-takers, these two do the same job, so running both at once is redundant. Pick the one that fits your platform and budget.
