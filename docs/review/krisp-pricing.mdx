# Krisp Pricing (2026)

<DisclosureNotice />

Krisp keeps pricing simple: a free plan that is good enough for many people, and a Pro plan that removes the limits. Exact prices change over time and by region, so the numbers below describe the plans, and you should confirm the current price on Krisp's own pricing page before you buy.

## Free plan

The free plan is the reason Krisp is easy to recommend:

- 60 minutes of noise cancellation per day
- Unlimited transcription
- A small number of AI meeting notes per day
- No credit card required

For occasional calls, this is often all you need.

## Pro plan

Pro is billed monthly or annually (annual is cheaper per month) and unlocks:

- Unlimited noise cancellation
- Unlimited AI summaries and action items
- Meeting history and recording
- Compliance features such as HIPAA and GDPR

## How to decide

- On calls all day in a noisy space? Pro pays for itself fast.
- A few calls a week? Start free and upgrade only if you hit the 60-minute daily cap.
