## Fix the audio first

Audio is where "professional" is won or lost.

- **Use a headset or a close mic.** It captures your voice and less of the room.
- **Kill the background noise.** Typing, fans, traffic, and other people talking all make you sound amateur, even when your words are fine.
- **Stop echo.** Move away from bare walls, or use a tool that removes echo on the call.

## Then tidy the picture

- **Light your face,** ideally from a window or a lamp in front of you, not behind.
- **Raise the camera to eye level** so you are not looking down at people.
- **Clear the frame** behind you, or use a simple, non-distracting background.
