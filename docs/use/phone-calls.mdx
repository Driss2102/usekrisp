# Krisp on phone and VoIP calls

<DisclosureNotice />

**Krisp cleans the audio on calls you make through apps on your computer, including softphones and VoIP tools.** It works the same way it does for meetings: it sits between your microphone and the calling app and removes background noise in both directions.

## On your computer

For VoIP and softphone apps (the kind sales teams and support agents use), the setup is the usual one:

1. Install and open Krisp, set your real mic and speaker, and turn on noise cancellation. See our [download guide](/docs/guides/how-to-download-krisp).
2. In your calling or softphone app's audio settings, choose **Krisp Microphone** and **Krisp Speaker**.

Because Krisp cleans both directions, it also reduces noise coming from the person on the other end, which is valuable on customer calls.

## On mobile

On phones, operating systems limit what apps can do to native carrier calls, so Krisp's mobile app focuses on recording, transcribing, and summarizing meetings rather than cleaning a regular phone call. For clean audio on the go, use Krisp with a calling app rather than the native dialer.

## Krisp vs KrispCall (not the same thing)

If you searched "krisp call," note that **Krisp** and **KrispCall** are different products. Krisp is the voice AI app covered here. KrispCall is a separate cloud phone system. This guide is about using Krisp for clean call audio.
