## Choose Krisp if…

Your problem is audio quality on calls. Avoma does nothing about background noise. Krisp cleans it in both directions, on any app, whatever team you are on.

## Choose Avoma if…

You are a sales or revenue team that needs conversation intelligence: CRM sync, coaching, scorecards, and deal insights across many calls. That is Avoma's home turf, not Krisp's.

## Or use both

They do not conflict: Krisp for clean audio, Avoma for the sales intelligence layer on top.
