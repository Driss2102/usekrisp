# Is Krisp worth it?

<DisclosureNotice />

**Yes, if you are on calls in a noisy space.** Krisp's bidirectional noise cancellation is the best plug-and-play way to sound clear, and the 7-day free trial lets you prove that on a real call before paying anything. If you only take the occasional quiet call, the free filters built into your calling app may be enough.

## When Krisp is worth paying for

- You are on video calls most of the day in a noisy space, so clean audio matters on nearly every call.
- You work in a noisy place: a shared office, a cafe, or a home with kids or pets.
- You want unlimited AI notes, recording, and meeting history alongside the clean audio.

In those cases, a paid plan pays for itself in saved "sorry, could you repeat that" moments.

## When it is not worth it

- You take only a few quiet calls a week, so the built-in suppression in your calling app is probably enough.
- Your one and only need is deep, searchable team notes. A dedicated note-taker goes further on that, and we compare them on our [alternatives](/docs/compare/) page.

## The free way to find out

You do not have to guess. Krisp's 7-day free trial needs no credit card and gives you full access. Use it on your next real call, in your actual environment, and you will know within one meeting whether the clarity is worth it for you.
