## Choose Krisp if…

Your problem is audio quality, or you take calls outside the browser (desktop apps, phone calls). Tactiq does nothing about background noise and lives in the browser. Krisp cleans audio everywhere.

## Choose Tactiq if…

You live in browser-based Google Meet or Zoom and mainly want fast live transcripts and AI prompts, without a bot. That is Tactiq's home turf.

## Or use both

They do not overlap much: <AffiliateLink href={KRISP_AFFILIATE_URL} variant="inline" location="inline-body">Krisp</AffiliateLink> for clean audio across every app, Tactiq for in-browser transcripts. They run together fine.
