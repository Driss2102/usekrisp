# Krisp on Cisco Webex

<DisclosureNotice />

Webex has its own noise removal, but Krisp is stronger, works in both directions, and behaves the same across every app you use, not just Webex. Setup takes a minute.

## Step by step

1. Install and open the Krisp app, set your real microphone and speaker inside it, and turn noise cancellation on.
2. In Webex, open **Settings**, then **Audio**.
3. Set your **Microphone** to **Krisp Microphone** and your **Speaker** to **Krisp Speaker**.
4. Start a call and confirm your audio is clean.
