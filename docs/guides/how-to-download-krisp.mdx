# How to download Krisp

<DisclosureNotice />

**Downloading Krisp takes a couple of minutes, and the 7-day free trial means you can start without paying or entering a card.** Here is how to get the app on Windows, Mac, and mobile, and how to set it up on your first call.

## Download on Windows or Mac

1. Go to the official Krisp site and download the desktop app for your system. Always download from Krisp directly, never from a third-party site.
2. Open the installer and follow the prompts. Krisp installs a virtual microphone and speaker so it can work with any app.
3. Sign in or create a free account.
4. In Krisp, select your real microphone and speaker, and turn on noise cancellation.

## Get it on mobile

Krisp has apps for iOS and Android for recording and summarizing meetings on the go. Search for "Krisp AI Note Taker" in the App Store or Google Play.

## First-time setup

Once installed, the key step is telling your calling app to use Krisp. In your app's audio settings, choose **Krisp Microphone** and **Krisp Speaker**. We have setup guides for [Zoom](/docs/use/zoom), [Microsoft Teams](/docs/use/microsoft-teams), [Google Meet](/docs/use/google-meet), and [Discord](/docs/use/discord).
