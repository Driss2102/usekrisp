## Choose Krisp if…

You use a laptop, a non-NVIDIA machine, or you want noise removal on incoming audio and notes as well.

## Choose NVIDIA Broadcast if…

You already own an RTX GPU and mainly want free outgoing noise removal for streaming.

## The practical difference

The choice usually comes down to two questions: what machine you are on, and whose noise you need gone.

NVIDIA Broadcast is tied to Windows and an RTX graphics card, and it focuses on your outgoing microphone. If you stream from a gaming PC, that is a great, free fit. Krisp runs on both Windows and Mac without a dedicated GPU, adds a virtual microphone that works in any calling app, and also cleans the noise coming from the people you are talking to, which is what matters when the barking dog is on the other end of the call. Krisp also keeps notes and action items, which NVIDIA Broadcast does not do.
