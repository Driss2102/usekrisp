# Krisp for Mac

<DisclosureNotice />

**Krisp runs natively on Mac, and setup is quick, but there is one macOS step that catches people out: microphone permissions.** Here is the full setup, including the part Apple hides in System Settings.

## Install and sign in

1. Download the Krisp app for Mac from the official site and open the installer. See our [download guide](/docs/guides/how-to-download-krisp) for the general steps.
2. Sign in or create a free account.
3. Krisp adds a virtual microphone and speaker that your calling apps can use.

## Grant microphone permission (the step people miss)

macOS blocks apps from using the mic until you allow it. If Krisp is not cleaning your audio, this is almost always why.

1. Open **System Settings**, then **Privacy and Security**, then **Microphone**.
2. Turn on the toggle for **Krisp**.
3. Restart Krisp if it was already open.

## Select Krisp in your calling app

In your app's audio settings, choose **Krisp Microphone** and **Krisp Speaker**. We have per-app guides for [Zoom](/docs/use/zoom), [Teams](/docs/use/microsoft-teams), and [Google Meet](/docs/use/google-meet).
