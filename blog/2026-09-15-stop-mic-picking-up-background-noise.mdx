## Start with placement and settings

- **Move the mic closer.** The closer it is to your mouth, the more it favours your voice over the room. A headset boom mic is ideal.
- **Lower the input gain.** A mic turned up too high strains to hear everything, including the background.
- **Use a directional mic if you have one.** Cardioid mics mostly hear what is in front of them.
- **Turn on your app's noise suppression** as a first layer.
