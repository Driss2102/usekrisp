# Krisp keyword research (2026) — Lot 2 planning

Compiled 2026-09-09 for usekrisp.com. Purpose: find where the ranking demand is,
in the markets Driss cares about (US, UK, Canada, Australia, Ireland + France),
and map each cluster to an existing page or a NEW page (Lot 2).

## Method & honesty note

No paid keyword tool (Ahrefs/SEMrush) was used, so there are **no invented exact
volumes**. Signals are real and cross-checked:
- Our own **Google Search Console + Bing** data (queries already getting impressions).
- **Live SERP inspection** (who ranks now = how competitive, and where a dedicated
  independent guide can win).
- The **Krisp content scrape** (`research/CONTENT-INTEL.md`, 543 posts) = what Krisp
  itself invests in = proven intent.
- Real **user questions** from G2 reviews and Reddit-style threads = AEO/FAQ gold.

Demand is rated qualitatively: 🔥 high, 🟢 solid, 🟡 niche. "Comp" = how hard the
SERP looks for a small new site.

## 1. Country strategy (read this first)

This is the most important decision, and it splits cleanly in two.

### English markets: US + UK + Canada + Australia + Ireland + NZ
**One English site ranks across ALL of them automatically.** Google serves
usekrisp.com to a UK or Canadian searcher the same way it does to a US one. You do
**not** need separate pages or hreflang for these. Two small, cheap wins:
- Use **British/Canadian spelling variants naturally** somewhere on the page:
  "noise cancelling" (UK/CA/AU) alongside "noise canceling" (US). Both already
  appear on the site; keep it that way. Same for "cancellation" (universal, safe).
- Krisp prices show in local currency on Krisp's side, so our "we don't quote a
  number, check the pricing page" rule already travels well internationally.

**Conclusion: for US/UK/CA/AU, there is nothing extra to do — just keep publishing
English content. The whole keyword map below already serves those 5+ countries.**

### France (+ Québec + Belgium + Switzerland romande)
This is the real decision. French searchers use **French queries** ("krisp avis",
"suppression de bruit micro", "réduction de bruit IA"). Our English pages will
**not** rank for those. Serving France means a **French section** (e.g. `/fr/…`)
with `hreflang` tags — a real project, not a toggle.

Good news from the live SERP: **French demand is real and the competition is
soft** — `krisp avis` returns generic review directories (lafabriquedunet,
comparatif-logiciels, blogdumoderateur), **no dedicated independent Krisp guide in
French exists.** There's even an open angle: **"krisp RGPD / alternative française"**
(a competitor, Agilotext, is already farming the data-hosted-in-France angle).

**Recommendation:** ship the full English Lot 2 first (serves 5+ countries at
zero extra cost), then decide on a **focused French mini-hub** (8–12 pages: avis,
tarifs, alternatives, RGPD, + top 3 "sur Zoom/Teams/Meet") as a separate phase.
Do NOT machine-translate the whole site — thin auto-translated locale pages can
drag the whole domain down (see seo-audit international rules). A small, genuinely
written French hub beats 80 translated stubs.

## 3. Cluster B — Troubleshooting (high volume + AEO citations) 🔧

rank (the SERP is old blog spam), and get **cited by AI answers** because they're
Q&A-shaped. High internal-linking value: a frustrated user fixes their issue, then
sees the affiliate CTA.

| krisp **crackling** / robotic / distorted audio | 🟢 | 🟢 | ❌ | section in the fix hub |
| krisp **microphone not working** / not detected | 🟢 | 🟡 | ⚠️ partial (`krisp-microphone`) | expand toward the fix angle |
| krisp with **AirPods / Bluetooth** headset | 🟢 | 🟢 | ❌ | **NEW** — was on the original plan (#7) |
| krisp keeps switching / **default device** prompt | 🟡 | 🟢 | ❌ | section — top user annoyance (also flagged in FR reviews) |

**Verdict: a "Krisp not working (2026 fix guide)" pillar + a dedicated "Discord
Krisp not working" page is the #2 Lot 2 priority.** Cheap to write, ranks fast,
AI-citable.
