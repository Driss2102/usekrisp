## Choose Teams' built-in if…

You work entirely in Microsoft Teams, the only issue is your own background noise, and you would rather not add another app. The "High" suppression setting handles a lot on your side at no extra cost.

## Choose Krisp if…

You call in more than one app, a noisy participant on the other end keeps ruining calls, or you want AI notes without a separate Copilot license. <AffiliateLink href={KRISP_AFFILIATE_URL} variant="inline" location="inline-intro">Krisp</AffiliateLink> cleans both directions and works the same across every calling app.

## Or use both

They coexist fine. A common setup is to let Krisp handle audio everywhere for consistency, and leave Teams' own suppression as a backup.
