## Choose Krisp if…

Background noise is the thing wrecking your calls, and you would rather not have a bot join. Krisp cleans noise both ways and records bot-free. The notes come along for the ride.

## Choose Fathom if…

You mainly want free, polished meeting notes with highlights and CRM sync, and noise is not your problem. Fathom's free note-taking is hard to beat on that axis.

## Or use both

Run Krisp for clean audio and bot-free capture, and Fathom where you want its free notes workflow. They do not conflict.
