# How to remove background noise from any call

<DisclosureNotice />

Background noise on calls comes from three places: your microphone picking up your room, the other person's noisy environment, and echo. Here are your options, from free to best.

## 1. Use your app's built-in suppression

Zoom, Teams, and Discord all have basic noise suppression in their audio settings. Turn it on first. It is free and helps with light noise, but it usually only cleans your outgoing audio and struggles with heavy noise.

## 2. Improve your setup

A headset with a close mic, a quieter room, and soft surfaces all reduce noise before software has to.

## 3. Use dedicated noise-cancellation software

For heavy noise, keyboard clatter, or noisy participants on the other end, dedicated software like Krisp works far better. It cleans both directions and runs on top of any app.
